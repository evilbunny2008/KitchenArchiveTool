I-Kitchen Archive Tool (KAT) ikuvumela ukuthi ubheke futhi useshe izindlela zokupheka ku-Nextcloud Cookbook yakho futhi upheke ngazo, ngisho noma ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi.

Izici
• Ngena ngohlelo lokusebenza lwe-Nextcloud ngaphandle kokufaka iphasiwedi yakho ku-KAT
• Izindlela zakho zokupheka zigcinwa kudivayisi futhi zivunyelaniswa ngemuva
• Sesha ngegama, igama elingukhiye, isithako noma inani labantu, uhlunge ngesigaba futhi uhlele uhlu
• Isibali-sikhathi sokupheka esakhelwe ngaphakathi
• Hlela futhi ususe izindlela zokupheka, noma uzikopishele kwenye i-akhawunti kanye nesithombe sazo
• Ngenisa indlela yokupheka cishe kunoma iyiphi iwebhusayithi ngokunamathisela isixhumanisi sayo
• Ama-akhawunti amaningi e-Nextcloud anokushintsha ngokushesha
• Itimu ekhanyayo nemnyama

Izidingo
• Iseva ye-Nextcloud efakwe uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-Cookbook
• Uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-Nextcloud lwe-Android olungene kuleyo seva

Ukungenisa izindlela zokupheka
Amawebhusayithi amaningi ezindlela zokupheka awanayo idatha i-Cookbook engayifunda yona ngokwayo. Ukuze ungenise kuwo, i-KAT ithumela kuphela ikheli lewebhu lendlela yokupheka kusevisi esizayo ku-recipes.odiousapps.com, efunda ikhasi bese ibuyisa indlela yokupheka. Bese i-KAT iyilayisha ku-Cookbook nge-akhawunti yakho. Isevisi ayilokothi ithole imininingwane yakho yokungena ku-Nextcloud noma enye idatha, futhi akukho okuthunyelwayo ngaphandle uma ungenisa indlela yokupheka.

I-KAT iyimfologo (fork) ye-Nextcloud Cookbook ka-MicMun futhi iyisofthiwe yamahhala ngaphansi kwe-GNU GPL v3 noma entsha kakhulu.
